
# 🔧 PHASE 0 — PLANNING EXECUTION (STEP-BY-STEP)

---

## STEP 1 — DEFINE BUILD GOAL

- Decide:
  - Daily usable car
  - Reliable turbo setup
- Avoid:
  - Overpower goals early

---

## STEP 2 — RESEARCH YOUR PLATFORM

- Study:
  - 4G94 engine limits
  - Turbo compatibility
- Identify safe boost range

---

## STEP 3 — BREAK PROJECT INTO PHASES

Order:

1. Maintenance
2. Turbo Build
3. Handling
4. Aesthetics

---

## STEP 4 — LIST ALL REQUIRED PARTS

- Write EVERYTHING before buying
- Categorize:
  - Engine
  - Suspension
  - Brakes
  - Cosmetic

---

## STEP 5 — REMOVE DUPLICATES

Example:

- Don’t buy stock clutch if upgrading later
- Don’t buy small brakes if planning big brake kit

---

## STEP 6 — DECIDE TURBO STRATEGY

- Low boost setup (safe)
- Stock internals
- Budget-friendly approach

---

## STEP 7 — BUDGET BREAKDOWN

- Allocate money per phase
- Keep buffer (~15–20%)

---

## STEP 8 — SOURCE PARTS

- Compare:
  - New vs used
  - Local vs imported
- Prioritize:
  - Critical components = high quality

---

## STEP 9 — CREATE PARTS TRACKER

- Use spreadsheet
- Track:
  - Price
  - Status
  - Delivery

---

## STEP 10 — PLAN WORKSPACE

- Ensure:
  - Enough space
  - Proper tools
  - Lighting

---

## STEP 11 — CREATE TIMELINE

- Plan realistic deadlines
- Avoid rushing builds

---

## STEP 12 — CONTENT PLANNING

- Decide:
  - Video sequence
  - Key moments to capture
- Plan storytelling

---
