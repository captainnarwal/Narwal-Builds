
# Aesthetics & Restoration Execution Plan

## Before Start

- Clean the car completely
- Plan paint areas
- Work in dust-free environment

## Step 1: Exterior Inspection

- Identify dents
- Check panel gaps
- Inspect paint damage

## Step 2: Dent Repair

- Sand damaged area
- Apply body filler
- Sand smooth

## Step 3: Panel Alignment

- Adjust doors, hood, bumpers

## Step 4: Paint Preparation

- Sand entire panel
- Apply primer
- Let dry

## Step 5: Paint Application

- Apply base coat (multiple layers)
- Let dry between coats
- Apply clear coat

## Step 6: Body Kit Install

- Test fit first
- Drill if required
- Secure using clips/adhesive

## Step 7: Lighting Upgrade

### Headlights:
- Install LEDs or projectors

### DRLs:
- Mount securely
- Connect via relay

## Step 8: Wheel Restoration

- Remove wheels
- Sand surface
- Paint + clear coat

## Step 9: Interior Restoration

### Seats:
- Clean or replace covers

### Roof Liner:
- Remove old fabric
- Apply new fabric

### Dashboard:
- Deep clean

## Step 10: Final Detailing

### Exterior:
- Wash
- Clay bar
- Polish
- Wax

### Interior:
- Vacuum
- Clean surfaces

## Step 11: Final Check

- Inspect paint finish, panel gaps, lights working
