
# Phase 0: Planning Execution

## Step 1: Define Build Goal

- Decide on daily usable car
- Choose reliable turbo setup
- Avoid setting overpower goals early

## Step 2: Research Your Platform

- Study 4G94 engine limits
- Check turbo compatibility
- Identify safe boost range

## Step 3: Break Project into Phases

Order:
1. Maintenance
2. Turbo Build
3. Handling
4. Aesthetics

## Step 4: List All Required Parts

- Write everything down before buying
- Categorize by:
  - Engine components
  - Suspension parts
  - Brake system
  - Cosmetic items

## Step 5: Remove Duplicates

Examples:
- Don't buy stock clutch if upgrading later
- Don't buy small brakes if planning big brake kit

## Step 6: Decide Turbo Strategy

- Low boost setup for safety
- Keep stock internals
- Budget-friendly approach

## Step 7: Budget Breakdown

- Allocate money per phase
- Keep buffer of ~15–20%

## Step 8: Source Parts

- Compare new vs used options
- Evaluate local vs imported
- Prioritize high quality for critical components

## Step 9: Create Parts Tracker

- Use spreadsheet for tracking
- Track price, status, and delivery

## Step 10: Plan Workspace

- Ensure enough space available
- Gather proper tools
- Set up adequate lighting

## Step 11: Create Timeline

- Map out phase durations
- Account for sourcing delays
- Build in flexibility for issues

- Plan realistic deadlines
- Avoid rushing builds
