
# 🔧 PHASE 3 — EXECUTION PLAN (AESTHETICS & RESTORATION)

---

## ⚠️ BEFORE START

- Clean the car completely
- Plan paint areas
- Work in dust-free environment

---

## STEP 1 — EXTERIOR INSPECTION

- Identify dents
- Check panel gaps
- Inspect paint damage

---

## STEP 2 — DENT REPAIR

- Sand damaged area
- Apply body filler
- Sand smooth

---

## STEP 3 — PANEL ALIGNMENT

- Adjust:
  - Doors
  - Hood
  - Bumpers

---

## STEP 4 — PAINT PREPARATION

- Sand entire panel
- Apply primer
- Let dry

---

## STEP 5 — PAINT APPLICATION

- Apply base coat (multiple layers)
- Let dry between coats
- Apply clear coat

---

## STEP 6 — BODY KIT INSTALL

- Test fit first
- Drill if required
- Secure using clips/adhesive

---

## STEP 7 — LIGHTING UPGRADE

### Headlights:

- Install LEDs or projectors

### DRLs:

- Mount securely
- Connect via relay

---

## STEP 8 — WHEEL RESTORATION

- Remove wheels
- Sand surface
- Paint + clear coat

---

## STEP 9 — INTERIOR RESTORATION

### Seats:

- Clean or replace covers

### Roof Liner:

- Remove old fabric
- Apply new fabric

### Dashboard:

- Deep clean

---

## STEP 10 — FINAL DETAILING

### Exterior:

- Wash
- Clay bar
- Polish
- Wax

### Interior:

- Vacuum
- Clean surfaces

---

## STEP 11 — FINAL CHECK

- Inspect:
  - Paint finish
  - Panel gaps
  - Lights working

---
