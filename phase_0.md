## 📋 Project Snapshot

**Vehicle:** 2011 Mitsubishi Cedia Sports Rally Edition
**Engine:** 4G94 2.0L Petrol, Manual Transmission
**Color:** Yellow (because why not?)
**Special Features:** MOMO Steering Wheel, OZ Racing Alloys
**Location:** India 🇮🇳
**Goal:** 160–200 HP turbocharged street car, reliable, fun, and not bankrupting me
**Budget:** ₹1.5L – ₹4L (INR)
**Timeline:** 6–12 months (currently in planning phase)

---

## 🏁 Why This Car?

- Strong cast iron block (boost capable)
- Excellent chassis & steering feel
- True sleeper platform
- Weak stock power → ideal for turbo build
- Rare, enthusiast-focused sedan in India

---

## 💡 Build Constraints

- ❌ No forged internals (engine stays unopened)
- ❌ Stock radiator retained
- ❌ Limited fuel upgrades (pump + injectors only)
- ✅ Stock 15” OZ Racing alloys retained
- ✅ Budget-conscious DIY build

## 🧠 Build Order & Reasoning

1. Maintenance (baseline reliability)
2. Handling + braking (control & safety)
3. Turbo system (power, only after above)
4. Aesthetics (final touches)

**Why this order?**

- Reliability first
- Power without control = unsafe
- Turbo exposes weak components

---

## 🔍 Technical Plan

### Engine

- Stock internals (~9.5:1 compression)
- Safe boost: 5–7 psi
- Must check: compression, oil condition, seals/leaks

### Turbo System

- Small turbo (TD04 / equivalent)
- 5–7 psi boost, fast spool, daily usable
- Custom exhaust manifold, intercooler, oil feed/return, wastegate, BOV, ECU tuning

### Fuel System

- High-flow fuel pump
- Larger injectors (no rail/FPR upgrade)

### Intake & Exhaust

- Free-flow intake
- Turbo-back exhaust manifold
- Stock exhaust and cat converter (for now)

### Cooling

- Stock radiator retained
- Closely monitor temps after turbo install

### Handling & Braking

**Brakes:**

- Performance pads, new rotors, brake fluid flush

**Suspension:**

- Polyurethane bushings, lowering springs/upgraded shocks

### Wheels & Tyres

- Stock 15” OZ Racing alloys retained
- UHP tyres (195/55 R15)

### Supporting Systems

- Transmission: inspect, fluid change
- Steering: check tie rods, rack ends
- Electricals: fix switches, bulbs, grounding
- Interior: clean, new roof liner
- Body: dent repair, paint correction
